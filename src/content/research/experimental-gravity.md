At Caltech's Experimental Gravity Group, I work on simulating photonic quantum states for applications in quantum computing and precision sensing at LIGO. My research focuses on how non-Gaussian states, such as Schrödinger cat states, can be generated from squeezed light using optical circuits involving beamsplitters and photon-counting measurements. I use QuTiP, Strawberry Fields, and MrMustard to model these circuits and study how realistic experimental imperfections, particularly optical loss and phase noise, affect the states we can produce. By exploring how different circuit configurations and experimental parameters influence state quality and generation probability, I aim to understand which quantum states can be practically generated and used in real experiments.
